Regulatory Safeguard (Statutory Clause)

The AICTE Approval Platform operates in strict accordance with the Approval Process Handbook and applicable AICTE Regulations. Automated alerts, deficiency recommendations, and workflow routing support the administrative process but shall not replace statutory scrutiny, physical inspections (EVC) or decisions reserved for the Competent Authority.

Land & Infrastructure Norms

  • Land Area: Minimum 2.5 Acres (Metro/State Capital) or 7.5 Acres (Rural/Other).
  • Title Deed: Clear ownership or registered lease agreement for minimum 30 years contiguous land.
  • Built-up Area: Instructional Area (45%), Administrative (15%), Amenities (15%), Circulation (25%).
  • Safety Clearances: Mandatory Fire Safety NOC & Structural Stability from Empanelled Engineers.

Faculty & Academic Norms

  • Faculty-Student Ratio (FSR): 1:20 for Undergraduate B.E/B.Tech; 1:15 for Postgraduate M.E/M.Tech.
  • Cadre Ratio: 1 Professor : 2 Associate Professors : 6 Assistant Professors.
  • Authentication: 100% Aadhaar & PAN seeding verified via AICTE Faculty Database.
  • Principal/Director: Mandatory Ph.D. with minimum 15 years experience and 8 research papers.
